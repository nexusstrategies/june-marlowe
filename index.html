I started painting at 61.

At the kitchen table, with nine tubes of paint and no idea what I was doing.

These days I paint most mornings. Flowers from the yard, whatever fruit is on the counter, and any front door that makes me stop the car.

Everything on this site is painted by me, one at a time. If one of them belongs in your house, I would love to send it to you.
